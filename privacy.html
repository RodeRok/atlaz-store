Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Atlaz Store is a small independent project run by one person in Slovenia.

What the app does

The Atlaz Store app lets the owner create and schedule his own Pins on his own Pinterest account, and view the statistics of those Pins.

What data it uses

Only the owner's own Pinterest account data: his boards, his own Pins and their statistics. It reaches them through Pinterest's official API, after the owner has signed in and approved access himself. The app does not collect, store or process personal data about other people, visitors or customers. It does not access other users' accounts, follow, message or comment on anyone. No data is sold or shared.

Where data is kept

The access token and the Pin data stay on the owner's own computer and are not shared with anyone.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or any tracking. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep ordinary server logs under GitHub's own privacy policy.

Links to other sites

Pins link to products on Amazon through affiliate links. Pinterest and Amazon have their own privacy policies and are responsible for the data they handle.

Your rights and contact

If you believe the app holds data about you, send a message to the Atlaz Store profile on Pinterest. Any such data will be deleted. Because the app only handles the owner's own account data, there should be none.

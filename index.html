Atlaz Store

Product recommendations for the US, the UK and Europe, shared as Pins on Pinterest.

What this is

Atlaz Store is a small independent project run by one person. It picks everyday products that are worth a look, such as things for the kitchen, home, outdoors, pets and tech, and shares them as Pins. Each Pin links to the product on the matching Amazon store: Amazon.com for the US, Amazon.co.uk for the UK and Amazon.de for Europe.

How the Pins are made

Pins are drafted with the help of software, including AI tools, and every Pin is reviewed and approved by the owner before it is published. Each Pin says plainly that it is an ad. The project does not use anyone else's account and does not send messages to anyone.

Affiliate disclosure

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. If you buy through a link in one of the Pins, the price you pay does not change, and a small commission goes to the project.
